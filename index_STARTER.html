Polar Bears

About My Animal

Adult males weigh 600 to 1,200 pounds and measure 8 to 10 feet long, while females are smaller at 400 to 700 pounds.Their fur looks white for camouflage, but each hair shaft is actually transparent and hollow to trap heat. Their skin underneath is jet black to absorb warmth from the sun.

Fun Facts

  • Beneath their thick coats, polar bears have jet-black skin to help absorb heat from the sun.
  • Their hair is actually transparent and hollow, reflecting visible light so it only looks white.
  • They can swim at speeds of 6 mph and go for days at a time across open water without resting.

Life Cycle

  1. Polar bear cubs are born in a snow den and depend completely on their mother for food and warmth.
  2. Cubs stay with their mother for about 2 years, learning to hunt, swim, and survive.
  3. Adult polar bears live mostly alone. They mate and females later give birth to new cubs, restarting the life cycle.
